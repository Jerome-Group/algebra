This is a coverage ledger, not a learning record. It maps the supplied 17-section study text to public concept pages and expandable notes. The public prose and examples are newly written; source files and extracted text remain ignored. Source numbering is retained here solely for verification.

The linked Alperin–Bell book supplies the matching 17-section structure. Each deeper note cites its section, result identifiers, printed page span and PDF page span. Section spans identify the full reading, not an assertion that every result appears on every page. Official course notes remain the primary references on retained course-derived concepts.

## Depth and explicit limits

Core definitions and substantive results have an explanation, statement or proof route on the mapped pages. They are not all expanded into standalone lessons. In particular the odd-order theorem is stated without its long proof; the full general linear character-value tables, detailed cohomology exercises, and individual exercise solutions remain in the linked access-controlled sources. The site gives the constructions, hypotheses and representative worked examples, not a reproduction of the private exercise solutions. The nine character-table examples have a consolidated construction guide; the existing live character lab computes the symmetric-group examples it lists, not every table in the guide.

The source audit includes every extracted numbered result and exercise identifier below. Exercise identifiers are reading routes, **not claims that every exercise is solved on the site**. This keeps the full syllabus discoverable without copying private learning records or turning the navigation into hundreds of fragments.

## Corrections checked against the mathematics

- Scalar character fibers at scalars other than one need not be subgroups; the kernel and scalar preimage are distinguished explicitly.
- Infinite index multiplication is justified by a coset-representative bijection, not cardinal cancellation.
- Special linear simplicity retains both small-field exceptions; projective and special linear groups are distinguished.
- Semisimple/radical statements specify finite dimension and unital modules. Maschke specifies a finite group and invertibility of its order.
- Isomorphism-theorem numbering varies; maps and quotients identify the result.
