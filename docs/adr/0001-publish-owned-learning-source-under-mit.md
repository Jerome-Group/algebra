# Publish owned learning source under MIT

The Owner wants students to inspect, run, adapt, and share the Abstract Algebra application, and selected
MIT for the owned source and documentation. This replaces the template's all-rights-reserved
placeholder; dependencies and vendored assets remain under their original terms.

The source snapshot comes from the existing Site revision `95215a69eeff93af7533f63d09459b2e6c3bb49c`. Course files, credentials,
private notes, and student data remain outside Git. Access-controlled notes links are retained
at the Owner's request; Drive permissions govern their targets. Ignore rules exclude PDFs,
common course-document formats, and private material directories before the first project commit.

The repository retains its Sites identity without republishing or changing the live deployment.
