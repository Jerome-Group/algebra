# Organise learning around connected mathematical concepts

Course and research labels explain provenance but provide a poor route through algebra. A quotient, an action or an invariant subspace reappears across several sources; separate course collections duplicated these concepts and hid their connections.

The learning interface uses mathematical subjects, collapsible chapter families and connected concepts. Stable legacy fragment identifiers remain aliases, so old links still resolve. Source provenance appears in citations, with printed and PDF page locations distinguished. Downloaded course documents and extracts remain outside Git.

A compact reading column uses tabs for understanding, examples, statements and proof guidance. Longer extensions are closed disclosures. The adjacent laboratory keeps the diagram above its controls and can be enlarged or minimised. This preserves space for mathematical reasoning while keeping experiments within reach.

KaTeX renders formulas in prose, controls, previews and SVG labels. WebMCP exposes concept navigation, reading state and the same controls as the visible interface. It does not create a second calculation engine.

## Consequences

- A concept can cite several sources; citations require a topic-specific check rather than automatic assignment by subject.
- The source coverage ledger distinguishes topic coverage from complete exercise solutions. Private learning records are not copied into public content.
- Legacy identifiers retain their old names internally, while visible navigation uses mathematical names.
- Compact diagrams need visual inspection at narrow widths. Typesetting validity alone cannot detect a clipped label or a misleading explanation.
- Registered laboratory controls mirror mounted React controls, so their identifiers must be refreshed after navigation.

## Revisit when

The library grows enough that subject search and chapter disclosures cease to give clear routes, or learners need saved study sequences and progress rather than a connected reference library.
