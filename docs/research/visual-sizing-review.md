# Visual sizing review — all laboratories

**Verdict: wrapping is incomplete; confirmed P2 visual defects remain.** Inspected all 150 initial laboratory states across 44 visual kinds at 320, 375 and 768px: 450 cases, one Chromium browser, built Worker `http://127.0.0.1:5175`. Prediction gates opened. No page errors; every outer page fits its viewport. That does not establish usable inner layout.

Machine-readable measurements, affected lesson lists and fixes: `outputs/visual-sizing-audit.json`. Screenshots: `outputs/visual-sizing-audit/`. No source, map or build edits.

## Confirmed findings

- **Nested compact scrolling hides models and controls: 94 lessons, all three widths.** `.exploration.compact .visual-panel > div` caps complete laboratory bodies at 340px (`app/laboratories.css:1056–1059`). Examples: `mh2220-generators` contains 2442px of content at 320; `cube-signed-permutations` contains 1730px. Users initially see only part of the model and must discover a separate vertical scrollbar to reach controls. `m3220-euclidean-768.png` visibly cuts its diagram between nodes. Remove the generic cap/inner vertical scrolling for laboratory routes and phones; preserve intentional table scrolling.
- **Oversized diagrams: 40 lessons at 768px.** Twenty-nine `Structure.tsx` concept maps render 533–546px high; ten cube/axis/cyclic projections render 520px; `ureca-character-bound-kernel` renders a 700px eigenvalue circle (`UrecaCharacterBound.tsx`). Unbounded width-driven scaling in `.structure-map svg` and `.vector-scene > svg` amplifies small mathematical objects. Use cohesive diagram-kind figure widths, intrinsic aspect ratios and an explicit enlargement state. Do not use a universal shrinking rule that makes labels unreadable. Representative screenshots: `m3220-euclidean-768.png`, `ureca-character-bound-kernel-768.png`.
- **Cube generator buttons crop on phones: eight cube lessons at 320 and 375px.** `.element-grid` is 244px wide but scrolls to 334px at 320. `app/responsive.css:91–93` replaces the safer grid with five `1fr` columns, retaining min-content widths. Last columns disappear behind the laboratory boundary. Confirmed after scrolling to the grid: `cube-signed-permutations-320-grid.png`. Use responsive auto-fit/minmax columns and wrapping labels; verify every button lies within its grid.
- **Paired selectors exceed their parent at 320px.** `groups-cauchy`, `mh2220-double-cosets`, `mh2220-simple-composition`: the second `Element b` custom chooser extends 8px beyond `.two-cols`. Stack paired controls on narrow screens and give controls/columns `min-width: 0` with bounded widths.
- **Diagram math labels shrink below readable type: 41 lessons, 100 viewport cases.** `SvgMath.tsx` places type inside SVG coordinates; whole-diagram scaling also scales its foreignObject labels. `mh2220-subgroups` labels measure approximately 7px at 320, 8.1px at 375 and 8.6px at 768; congruence labels measure 6.7–8.3px. Keep rendered labels readable through mobile-specific geometry/type or adjacent explicit legends/tables; assess text separately from the figure box.

## Interpretation and limits

Raw SVG foreignObject box flags do not prove glyph clipping: boxes can extend farther than painted text. Long mathematical formulas can legitimately scroll horizontally. Neither is automatically counted as a confirmed defect above. No canvas appears in the observed initial states. Screenshots independently confirmed representative clipping/size defects; geometry covered every lesson. Expanded modes, all parameter combinations, zoomed text and deployment behavior remain outside this sizing sweep. Rerun these checks against the rebuilt artifact after fixes; the previous release-suite pass does not close these findings.
